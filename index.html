Tandem malfunction codes

Look up the malfunction code a t:slim X2 or Mobi pump shows, for example 12-0x2071.

A pump malfunction stops insulin delivery. If your pump shows one, contact Tandem Technical Support and follow the backup plan from your care team for taking insulin another way. This page is not medical advice and is not affiliated with Tandem Diabetes Care.

Names and descriptions come from pumpX2 () and TandemKit (), which agree.
